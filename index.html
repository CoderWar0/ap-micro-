🎯 Top Exam Tips for May 4, 2026

  • Draw graphs every time. Nearly every FRQ requires a correctly labeled graph (S/D, Cost Curves, MR/MC). Practice drawing them fast.
  • Label axes. Unlabeled axes cost you points even if the shape is correct.
  • Know the "why." MCQ frequently asks you to explain *why* a curve shifts, not just which direction.
  • Use MR = MC rule for ALL profit-maximizing firms — perfect competition, monopoly, monopolistic competition, and oligopoly.
  • Factor markets mirror product markets. MRP = MFC is just MR = MC applied to inputs.
  • Government intervention = deadweight loss. Taxes, price floors/ceilings, monopoly — all create DWL triangles.
  • A four-function calculator is permitted. Practice PED, PES, and TR/TC/profit calculations by hand.
